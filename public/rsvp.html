Special Evenings

These evenings are our thank-you to Veterans, Active Duty Military, First Responders and Teachers. Students are welcome at the Final Dress Rehearsal. You may bring one guest.

Each dinner is served at 6:00 PM before that night's curtain, in the same building. Dinner seats 80. The house seats 240. For either night, come to dinner and the show, dinner only, or the show only — whichever fits. If a dinner fills up, the show is still yours. Leave a night blank if you aren't coming to it.
